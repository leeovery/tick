### 1. The bound on the `tick-core` amendment is missing, and the passage it is owed on sits beside two more shapes this work replaces

**Type**: Incomplete coverage
**Spec Reference**: §12.2 — the `v1` / `tick-core` row ("States 'long text fields get their own unstructured sections' as a principle and prints the indented description block as its worked example (`sed -n '693,714p' .workflows/v1/specification/tick-core/specification.md`)"), and the closing paragraph: "**Corrections are made by judgement, not as a blanket rewrite.** Specifications are forever documents that do not churn out of the knowledge base, and the facility for amending them exists, so a correction can be made wherever something is obviously wrong. But this specification supersedes those decisions regardless, so correcting them is not obligatory. Where a point is plainly and load-bearingly wrong, amend it; otherwise let supersession carry it." Also §12.2's account of the corrigendum route: "a dated corrigendum entry records what the document used to claim **and what is true instead**, and the document is re-indexed. The re-index is the point of the route — a specification's content stays live in the knowledge base at full confidence."
**Plan Reference**: Phase 1, task `free-text-round-trip-1-3` (Description becomes one TOON-quoted value) — Context, final paragraph
**Move**: settled
**Change Type**: update-task

**Problem**:
Another team's completed specification gets edited wider than this work was allowed to edit it. `tick-core` is a forever document that stays live in the knowledge base at full confidence, and the specification decided exactly one point in it is wrong enough to amend: the "long text fields get their own unstructured sections" principle and the indented description block it prints as that principle's worked example. Everything else superseded in that document is explicitly left to supersession — "correcting them is not obligatory".

The plan tells the session to amend and does not tell it where to stop. The passage it points at is not isolated: the line range the plan reproduces from the specification (`693,714p`) has the malformed `stats{total,open,in_progress,done,cancelled,ready,blocked}:` header sitting inside it at `tick-core/specification.md:700`, and the equally malformed `task{id,title,status,priority,parent,created,updated}:` header sixteen lines above at `:684`. Both are shapes this very plan replaces — Phase 3 task `free-text-round-trip-3-1` and Phase 1 task `free-text-round-trip-1-1` — so a session running the corrigendum with this work fresh in mind sees three wrong things where the specification sanctioned one, and the natural move is to fix the block. That is the blanket rewrite §12.2 names and refuses.

The sibling correction already carries the bound. Task `free-text-round-trip-2-6` states both limits on the `auto-cascade-parent-status` amendment — the §7.6 carve-out and "nothing else in that document is edited" — and gives the corrigendum route in full, including that the entry records what is true instead and why the re-index is the point of it. The `tick-core` note carries neither the bound nor those two clauses, so the two amendments run to different rules for no reason a reader can find.

**Proposal**:
Carry §12.2's bound into the `tick-core` note and bring its account of the route level with the sibling's. The specification settles every clause: the scope limit is "corrections are made by judgement, not as a blanket rewrite" together with "correcting them is not obligatory", the corrigendum entry records "what the document used to claim and what is true instead", and the re-index is "the point of the route" because the content stays live at full confidence. Naming the two neighbouring headers is the specification's own scoping read against the document it points at — `tick-core:684` and `tick-core:700` are shapes this work replaces that §12.2's "What is wrong" column does not name, and the second falls inside the line range the plan reproduces, which is what makes the bound load-bearing rather than decorative. The form is the plan's own: `free-text-round-trip-2-6`'s paragraph is the template. No Do step, acceptance criterion or test changes, the Spec Reference already carries §12.2, and the amendment stays a Context note rather than task work because `task-design.md` forbids a task editing another work unit's artifact under `.workflows/`.
